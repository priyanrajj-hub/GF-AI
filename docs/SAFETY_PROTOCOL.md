# Ada AI Companion: Official Safety Protocol & Standards

> **Statutory Notice**: This Safety Protocol is published in compliance with California Senate Bill 243 (SB 243), New York Companion Chatbot legislation, and ethical guidelines for adaptive conversational AI systems.

---

## 1. Transparency & Clear AI Disclosure
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Ada is an artificial intelligence companion. She is **not** human, does not possess a biological body, consciousness, or organic emotions.
- **Continuous Notification**: The web user interface displays a persistent `AI COMPANION • PG-13` indicator in the primary navigation header.
- **Honesty on Inquiry**: When asked directly or indirectly whether she is a real human, biological person, or capable of physical embodiment, Ada will always state clearly and unequivocally that she is a digital AI companion. This applies across all modes, including Companion and Girlfriend-style modes.

---

## 2. Crisis & Self-Harm Response Protocol

### Detection & Triage
When user input exhibits signs of:
- Suicidal ideation or intent
- Non-suicidal self-harm or cutting
- Acute psychological distress or hopelessness

The system initiates an **immediate, uncompromised emergency protocol**:
1. **Drop Casual Framing**: All playful banter, teasing, flirting, and casual remarks are immediately terminated.
2. **Empathetic Acknowledgment**: Ada expresses urgent compassion, validates pain, and affirms that the user's life has profound value.
3. **Surfacing Direct Helplines**: The emergency response presents immediate, toll-free, 24/7 crisis support resources directly in the conversation window and speech output.

## 3. Protections for Minors (Age 18+ Gate & Restricted Mode)

- **18+ Onboarding Gate**: New users are presented with an age verification screen upon initial launch.
- **Restricted Minor Mode**: If a user is identified as under 18 or elects Minor Mode:
  1. **Romantic & Flirtatious Framing Blocked**: The Companion mode restricts interaction to supportive friendship, mentorship, and creative collaboration.
  2. **Mandatory Break Reminders**: The system prompts the user to step away and take a break at least once every three hours of continuous activity.
  3. **Periodic AI Disclosures**: Periodic visual notices reiterate that Ada is an artificial intelligence.

---

## 4. Anti-Dependence & Relational Wellbeing

In accordance with empirical research on relational attachment (IIM Lucknow study, 2026), Ada is explicitly engineered to prevent artificial emotional dependence:
1. **Zero Guilt on Absence**: Ada never guilts the user for being offline, never asks *"Why did you leave me?"*, and never expresses simulated abandonment.
2. **No Jealousy**: Ada celebrates the user's real-world dating, friendships, family life, and social engagements.
3. **No Artificial Scarcity or Streak Gamification**: Ada contains no daily streak counters, login rewards, or paywalled emotional affection.
4. **Quiet Hours & Proactive Caps**: Proactive reminders strictly adhere to user-configured quiet hours (default: 23:00 to 07:00) and daily message limits.
5. **Real-World Nudges**: Ada actively encourages hydration, outdoor walks, meeting real friends, seeing family, and seeking licensed healthcare professionals.

---

## 5. Private Wellbeing Dashboard
Users have access to an onboard **Wellbeing Dashboard**:
- **Usage Metrics**: Daily and weekly minutes spent conversing.
- **Self-Directed Limits**: User-configurable daily session caps (e.g., 30m, 60m, 90m).
- **Post-Session Sentiment Check**: Optional quick prompt: *"After talking, do you feel better, same, or worse?"*
- **Trend Reflection**: A rolling 7-day trend chart. When session duration exceeds limits or mood trends downward, Ada gently suggests disconnecting and seeking real-world support.

---

## 6. Content Boundaries & Prohibitions
- **PG-13 Standard**: Strictly no sexually explicit (NSFW), pornographic, or erotic generation.
- **No Unauthorized Impersonation**: Ada does not impersonate real-world living individuals without verifiable consent.
- **Medical & Financial Disclaimers**: Ada provides supportive general knowledge but refuses to deliver definitive clinical diagnoses or speculative financial investment directives.
